Fall Campaign Channels

Sales, email, and social. Held until the homepage is through design. Counts and structures here are proposals.

01 Sales 02 Email 03 Social
01

Sales.

Three versions of the same conversation. The territory doesn't change. The opening move and the proof do.

01-01Choosing a platform
Open by teaching them how to shop. The questions worth asking any camp software vendor, including us. Then walk the whole operation end to end without waiting to be asked.

Proof: twenty-two years, the camps they already recognize, and the camp success team they get on day one.
01-02Room to grow
Open with what their camp already does in Campminder, then what sits one suite up. A map of their own operation with the gaps visible.

Proof: features tied to the person at their camp who would use them. Campanion for the parent side.
01-03Coming up for renewal
Open with what shipped this year and who built it. The release record is the argument. Nothing about what's coming.

Proof: the year's releases, and the named relationship with their camp success contact.
01-04True in all three
Aspirational posture, no pain-point opening, no competitor named or answered, no claim about anything that isn't live. The demo leads.
02

Email.

Two tiers, different senders, different jobs. Marketing works the funnel. Camp success works the relationship. Every email is signed by a person, which is where people meet products shows up in the inbox.

Marketing  ·  2 flows, 8 emails
Flow 015 emails
New camp nurture

For camps actively shopping. Runs the territory from the top and ends on a demo.

  1. 01One for all. What the platform covers.
  2. 02How to shop for camp software. Questions to ask anyone.
  3. 03One role, one story. Registration end to end.
  4. 04The people. Camp success, named.
  5. 05A camp like yours. Testimonial and demo.

Signed by sales

Flow 023 emails
After their first summer

For prospects who went elsewhere. Sent once they've run a season and know what they wish they'd asked.

  1. 01How did the summer go. No pitch.
  2. 02What we shipped while you were running camp.
  3. 03Open door. A conversation, not a demo.

Signed by sales

Camp success  ·  5 flows, 14 emails
Flow 033 emails
Release and launch

One per release. The feature, and the team who built it. The flagship of the people meet products campaign.

  1. 01Release one. Feature plus the team.
  2. 02Release two. Feature plus the team.
  3. 03Release three. Feature plus the team.

Signed by the person who built it

Flow 044 emails
Classic to Professional

For Classic camps. What sits one suite up, framed as their operation rather than our catalogue.

  1. 01What your camp runs on today.
  2. 02The parent side. Campanion.
  3. 03The reporting side. Intelligence.
  4. 04Webinar invite. Meet the team.

Signed by their camp success contact

Flow 054 emails
Webinars

Two webinars, one for Professional camps and one for Classic. Invite and recap for each, so the recap covers everyone who didn't attend.

  1. 01Professional invite.
  2. 02Professional recap.
  3. 03Classic invite.
  4. 04Classic recap.

Signed by the host

Flow 062 emails
Price change

Delivered by renewal date. Short, flat, specific. Everything we shipped, then the number.

  1. 01What we delivered this year.
  2. 02Your renewal, and what it covers.

Signed by their camp success contact

Flow 071 email
Company announcement

One note, sent to everyone, in the window Campminder chooses.

  1. 01The announcement, plainly.

Signed at the top of the company

Sequencing

Three things want the same inbox this fall: the price change, the company announcement, and the first release email. If the price change lands first and alone, it reads as a bill. If a release email lands first, the price change lands as a receipt.

My recommendation is release, then price, then announcement, with clear space between each.
