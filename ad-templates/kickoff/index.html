Scope of work

We start with this kickoff. From there the templates are designed in one pass, and we go through them together at a walkthrough meeting.

01Kickoff
Includes
  • Kickoff meeting. With everyone on your project team, covering:
    • The scope of work and the schedule
    • Your current ads and the brand manual
02Ad templates

One type-only visual system for Campminder ads.

Includes
  • Creative direction. The concept for the visual system.
  • Visual system. Type, color, CTA button, logo placement, backgrounds.
  • Walkthrough meeting. The finished templates, shown together.
You get
  • Ad templates. At square, tall, and wide.
  • Safe areas. Marked on each template.
  • Background exports. PNG, at each size.
  • Figma file. With the ad templates.
Outside this scope

Rounds of edits, imagery, ad copy, additional templates or sizes, Canva builds, video, UltraCamp.

The templates ship with placeholder copy. Revisions after delivery are quoted before any work starts, and anything above can be added with a separate proposal.

Your project team

Everyone approving direction on the templates stays involved from kickoff through completion. If someone new joins partway through and their input changes approved direction, that becomes a change in scope and I'll quote the adjustment before the project continues.

Project team. Imani and Snug
