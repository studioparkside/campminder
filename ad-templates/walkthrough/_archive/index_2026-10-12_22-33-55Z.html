Project recap

Hi Snug and Imani, the ad templates are below. You'll see one type-only visual system on the Campminder brand, in a light version and a dark version at every size.

02

Ad system

02-01Templates
02-02Elements

Headline

Anchored to the top left corner of the safe area, with a purple lead-in. Same position on every ad.

Body copy

Optional. About 80% of the headline width.

Button

Optional. Below the body copy, in the same position on every ad. Purple gradient, white text.

Logo

On every ad. Bottom left corner of the safe area, sized to the frame.

Background

A gradient, light or dark.

Safe area

Everything stays inside the dashed line, one margin in from the edge.

02-03Type
Typeface
General Sans.
Headline
Bold, 104 px. 136 px on Wide (YouTube). Line height 100%, letter spacing −2%.
Body copy
Medium, 36 px. Line height 130%.
Button
Semibold, 28 px.

Layout

Every template is built on the same grid. The dashed line is the safe area. Keep all active content inside it.

64 px on every size.
Gutter
16 px on every size.
Grid
12 columns and 12 rows inside the margin.

The margin box.
