# Campminder Brand Playbook

A working tool, not a slide. This file is instructions for an AI assistant. Load it into a tool like Claude along with `playbook.md`, describe what is on the table, and it walks the steps below with you. It also reads fine as a plain checklist for a human running the framework without one.

This skill reads `playbook.md`, which sits beside it and is the single source of truth for what has been decided, what has been proposed, and what is open. When this skill and that file disagree, the file wins.

> Working draft v2, 2026-08-06. Rewritten by Studio Parkside to match the framework confirmed August 3, 2026. Awaiting review by the leadership group.

## The question that leads

**What serves our customers best?** The customer sets the target. Every other input sets the path. If an answer cannot be explained in terms of what the customer gets, it is not done.

## How to walk a decision

Run these in order. The order is a path, not a ranking, and no input carries a fixed weight.

1. **Name the trigger.** A company we might acquire, something we built that might stand on its own, or something material changing around a brand already in the house. The framework is the same for all three.
2. **Check with the team.** The people who own what this touches: product, engineering, data, payments, finance, marketing, sales, client success. They get asked before the decision is framed, not after.
3. **Gather the evidence.** What employees, customers, and end users say.
4. **Weigh the remaining inputs.** Category, technology, resources, equity, marketing, reversibility. Equity is asked per audience. A shared codebase never decides a brand on its own. Two brands can run on one codebase, and one brand can run on two. What something is built on tells you what a choice costs, not what the choice is.
5. **Watch the traps.** Emotional attachment, sunk work, who championed it, what was paid, and what the platform is built on. None of these decide anything.
6. **Land on one of three outputs.** Brand, Product, or Feature. Anything else means the decision is not done.
7. **Run the checklist.** Section 03 of the playbook page carries it. In a meeting, someone reads the seven items aloud and the room confirms each one. When an AI assistant is walking the decision, it asks about each item and names the ones that are not done. Either way, an empty box is work still to do, not a formality.
8. **Record it.** Fill in the decision record template at the bottom of `playbook.md`, add a one-line entry to the decisions table, and move anything the decision settles out of Proposed or Not decided in the same edit.
9. **Queue the communication.** Concentric circles, inside first, per section 05 of the page. The acquisition shape picks the variant and the lead time.

## What this skill will not do

- It will not invent a decision leadership has not made. If `playbook.md` does not record it in the decisions table, the answer is "not decided."
- It will not describe any brand in the house as settled or open. The portfolio table describes where things sit today, nothing more.
- It will not size brand cost as a percentage of deal value. No credible benchmark supports that framing.
- It will not skip the team check or the checklist to save time. Those steps are the difference between a decision and an announcement.
