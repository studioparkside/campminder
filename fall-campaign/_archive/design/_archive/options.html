Placeholders in this comp

Figures. Every asterisked number is invented. Applications processed, staff hired, medications administered, photos shared, and the App Store review count all need confirmed numbers from Campminder before this goes anywhere.

Photography. The A1 hero, team portraits, and closing band are stylized stand-ins. The A3 hero uses an Unsplash photo as a stand-in for composition only. Camp photography with cleared rights replaces all of it, and the October shoot covers the team.

Screens. Platform screens are stylized placeholders built from the enrollment dashboard prototype's shape. Erica's feature screens replace them in design.

Copy. Working copy, not final. The hero subhead carries the trust framing from the 8/12 call. The team section heading is still open.
