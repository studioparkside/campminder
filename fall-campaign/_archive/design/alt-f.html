Alternate F Spine

One vertical hairline runs the full height of the page and everything hangs off it. Sections are large fields of ground with very little in them, and content sits at the bottom of its cell rather than in the middle. Numbered notes run in the outer margins in Spezia SemiMono. Headlines are smaller than they would normally be, because the confidence comes from the air around them rather than the size of the type. Ground is the approved dark plum.
