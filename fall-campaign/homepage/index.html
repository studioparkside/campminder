Campminder Fall Campaign Homepage

This homepage concept is built on the territory for the fall campaign. The language, design, layout, and everything on the page are starting points only. After we review it, I’ll put together a project brief to go alongside it for Groundwrk to build from.

Option 1

The camp and the platform in one frame. An update to the direction we looked at last week, with the technology sitting in the camp environment.

Option 2

A gradient that opens on the words, then the platform. The dashboard sits directly under the headline, so the range of what Campminder does is at the top of the page.

Option 3

A gradient with the platform cropped into it. Pieces of the product run off the right edge instead of sitting in a frame. The row could slide to the left on scroll, so the longer someone stays on it the more of the platform they see.

Notes.

01
The page design builds on an editorial structure, with oversized headlines, hairline rules, and rounded product screens to match the platform. From a design perspective, the brand reads as established and trustworthy.
02
Modular layout. Everything under the hero is a self-contained section. The order can change as we learn what performs, and what’s on the page can change as new features are revealed.
03
Fonts. Spezia Narrow is the display typeface. General Sans is used for reading copy and the product screens. Both need to be licensed before launch.
04
All copy is placeholder. It’s here to show structure and length. Every headline, number, and date needs Campminder to confirm or correct it.
05
Select one option to move forward. Pick the hero direction you want to share with Groundwrk for the homepage build.
06
All imagery is for demonstration purposes only. Platform screens, camp imagery, and team photos are supplied by Campminder and shared with Groundwrk for the homepage build.
