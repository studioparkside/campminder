01

Territory.

The territory is the conceptual foundation for every piece of communication in the campaign. It extends through the homepage, the sales conversation, the emails, and the social posts, so all of them tell one story.

This territory says that Campminder is one comprehensive platform covering every role at a camp, with the people and the intelligence behind it.

One for all.

One platform for everyone your camp connects to.

Directors fill their camp. Staff get hired and show up ready. Parents register every kid in one pass. Registration, staffing, health, communication, and the business behind it all, with Campminder Intelligence running underneath and a camp success team that has your back.

Take the tour, or we'll walk you through it.

Unpack possible.Book a demo

07

Sales narrative.

An extension of your team. Below, a range of narratives for your sales and camp success teams to build from in conversations with current and future customers.

07-01New
Choosing a platform
  • Here’s what to ask any vendor you talk to, including us.
  • Let me walk you through the whole operation, end to end.
  • You already know the camps that are running on this.
  • You get a camp success team on day one.
07-02Upgrade
Room to grow
  • Here’s what your camp does in Campminder today.
  • Here’s what sits one suite up.
  • This one’s for your registrar. This one’s for your nurse.
  • Campanion is the piece your families see.
07-03Renewing soon
Ready for renewal
  • Here’s everything that shipped this year.
  • Here’s who built it.
  • You know your camp success contact by name.
  • I’m not going to talk about what’s coming.
07-04Objection
You’ve announced things before and not delivered
  • You’re right. We did that.
  • Here’s the release record, month by month.
  • I’m not going to tell you what’s next.
  • Judge us on what’s already in your hands.
07-05Objection
It’s hard to use, and it looks like older software
  • Let me show you what’s new rather than describe it.
  • Here’s what we haven’t modernized yet.
  • Onboarding comes with a person who runs it with you.
  • You won’t be learning this on your own.
07-06Objection
I prefer one of the competition’s platforms
  • Fair. Let me show you what’s live here.
  • I’m not going to talk about anyone else.
  • We’ve been around twenty-five years, and we’re not going anywhere.
  • Ask them the same questions you asked me.

Assets.

Here's what we need to start moving forward.

08-01ASAP
Confirm the wireframe

What's missing, wrong, or shouldn't be there. Everything after this builds on it.

08-02ASAP
Confirm product details

The page gets designed around the features that are certain to be ready, described the way your team describes them internally. Any nuance or detail that Groundwrk and I will need is better surfaced now than after design starts.

08-03ASAP
Camp photography

Camp photos with usage rights cleared, for the hero and the team block.

08-04Soon
Customer testimonials

On the record, from long-tenure camps. More than one, so there's a choice to make.

08-05Soon
The pricing decision

Whether pricing goes on the page, and if so, a starting number per suite. Better settled before design than after.
