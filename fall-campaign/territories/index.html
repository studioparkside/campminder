01

Working session.

Three points closed the session. Each territory on this page was built against them.

The holistic approach
The comprehensive nature of not only the Campminder platform but the relationship. Everything a camp runs on, and the people who run it alongside them.
Aspirational over pain points
Empowering camps to do more. We're not here to remind our customers how hard their job is. They know. We show what they get.
Show, don't tell
The product itself on the homepage, explained plainly. Transparency over claims. Screens, numbers, and words a camp director can act on.

05

Guardrails.

Set in the working session. These hold no matter which territory leads.

05-01No pain-point selling
We don't remind directors their job is hard. They know. We show what they get.
Via Snug and Frank
05-02No future tense
If it isn't live, it isn't on the page. Announcing ahead of delivery is what damaged trust.
Via Charles
05-03No speed claims
Faster gets proven by shipping, then shown. Not said first.
Via Frank
05-04No running against anyone
We don't name competitors or answer them. We state what we stand for and let our customers make the decision.
Via Paul
05-05No tech talk without a use
Modern under the hood, plain on the page. Technical language earns its place only when a camp director can act on it.
Via Mya
