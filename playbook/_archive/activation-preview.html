03

One parent. Independent brands.

In a House of Brands, the parent company stands behind fully independent brands. Customers buy from the name they know and trust. The parent signals shared ownership, shared talent, and shared ambition. PepsiCo and Meta run this way.

Three reasons this is the right model for Campminder.

01

Campminder has real equity.

Customers keep the name they trust. The parent adds a layer above Campminder instead of replacing it.

02

The brands serve different markets.

A Campminder customer and a Swimtopia customer have different needs and different reasons to buy. Each brand speaks to its own market without compromise.

03

The company already works this way.

Shared engineering, shared finance, and shared leadership already run beneath multiple product names. The model gives that structure a name and makes it visible.

Suggested seed · a draft to react to

We are one company behind several brands. Each brand serves its own community under its own name. Behind all of them sit the same people, the same engineering, and the same standard of care. We keep the names our customers trust.

06

Brand, product, or feature.

Every acquisition and every new build gets the same first question. What is this to the portfolio? The framework returns one of four answers, and the answer sets the name, the identity, and the budget.

Brand

Its own name, identity, and audience. Joins the house.

Sub-brand

Rides with an existing brand and borrows its trust.

Standalone product or platform

Its own product, sold under an existing brand.

Feature

Folds into a current platform. No public brand of its own.

Suggested criteria · to sharpen together

Is there equity worth protecting? Does the audience overlap with a brand we already have? Does the product stay separate or merge into a current platform? Can we resource it as a brand once we own it? Paul set the bar for all of these: every answer rooted in data and precedent.

Gazebo and Campanion are the first use cases. UltraCamp follows once the customer research is in.

Calls that could get made by default get a decision record instead, so they are made by an owner on purpose. The first one is ready: the brand name in the product, hardcoded or tokenized.
